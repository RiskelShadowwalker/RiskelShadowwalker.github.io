Undertaker fan page!

Who is the Undertaker?:

  • Professional Wrestler
  • Real name: Mark William Calaway
  • Born: March 24, 1965, in Houston, Texas
  • Active Years: 1987 to 2020
  • Total Matches: 2502

Pre Undertaker (1986-1990)

The early years (1986–1989):

  • Began training in 1986 under Buzz Sawyer
  • First match on June 26th 1987 in WCCW
    • Already with Percival "Percy" Pringle (Paul Bearer)
  • Wrestled under the gimmick "The Commando" in 1988 for Central Illinois Wrestling and Southern Championship Wrestling
  • Joined Continental Wrestling Association in 1988 as "Master Of Pain"
    • Managed by Dutch Mantel
    • Won his first championship against Jerry Lawler (USWA Unified World Heavyweight Champion)
      • Lost to Lawler again 3 weeks later
    • Returned as "The Punisher" winning the USWA Texas Heavyweight Championship after Erik Embry forfeited the title

The year in WCW (1989–1990):

  • Joined World Championship Wrestling as "Mean Mark Callous"
    • Joined The Skyscrapers tag team to replace injured Sid Vicious
    • After Skyscraper break up guided by Paul E. Dangerously(Paul Heyman)
  • Made efforts to join the WWF (now WWE) after being told by the company booker Ole Anderson that nobody would ever pay money to see him wrestle
    • Gave notice to WCW immediately before the interview to join the WWF with Vince McMahon even happened
    • McMahon initially didn't offer him a job but called days later saying he had an idea for a Wrestler portraying an old west undertaker but until now hasn't found an appropriate wrestler to play the part
  • Last WCW match on September 7th
  • During his WCW he also Wrestled some matches in New Japan Pro Wrestling(NJPW) as "Punisher" Dice Morgan
  • Returned briefly to USWA after leaving WCW

Time in WWF/WWE (1990 - today)

Debut of the Undertaker (1990–1991):

  • Originally titled "Kane the Undertaker"
  • Was not fully satisfied with the gimmik at first but accepted because everything was better then some laughingstock gimmik like The Gobbledy Gooker
  • Described as "The Old West Mortician
  • Debut on November 19, 1990 at WWF Superstars winning his first match against Mario Mancini
    • Was only taped but didn't air until December 15, 1990
    • Also had a match on November 20th against Rick Sampson airing December 9, 1990
  • Televised debut live at Survivor Series on November 22, 1990
    • Joined as the heel mystery partner of Ted DiBiase's (The Million Dollar Man)
